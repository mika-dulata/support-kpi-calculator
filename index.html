How it works

The model, in plain words

Human queue

Conversations the bot hands over, plus the "contained" ones that weren't solved and come back. That second group is the one most dashboards forget.

Erlang C

The standard queueing formula for contact centres. Given arrivals, handle time and agents, it estimates the share answered within your target. It assumes nobody hangs up while waiting, so it is a little pessimistic on wait and optimistic on nothing else.

Real containment

Reported containment times the verified solved rate. If the bot "contains" 60% and 70% of that is solved, the bot really resolves 42%. That's the number to put in front of leadership.

Cost per verified resolution

Agent hours plus AI usage, divided by problems actually solved. Conversations handled by a person are counted as 95% solved. Divide by closed conversations instead and automation always looks cheaper than it is.

Concurrency

Two chats at once does not mean double speed. Here it's a plain divisor of handle time, which flatters chat. For security or complaint queues, set it to 1.

What it leaves out

Abandonment, skills routing, languages, time zones and P1 queues. A security queue should be sized on its own, from handoff volume by hour, not from the average.
